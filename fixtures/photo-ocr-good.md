# What good looks like — a lesson with a page that could not be read

The whole point of this fixture is the gap. One page of the student's photographs had no readable
text, and the extraction says so.

- **Nothing is invented across the gap.** No content is written for the unread page, and no
  sentence is constructed that implies continuity through it.
- **An open question names it**: "one of your pages could not be read — it sits between amplitude
  and phase difference. What was on it?" with what to check and where.
- The readable material is rebuilt properly: transverse vs longitudinal as a comparison table,
  v = fλ as a three-part formula (v in m s⁻¹, f in Hz, λ in m), wavelength and phase difference
  defined, a wave diagram labelled with amplitude and wavelength.
- Intensity ∝ amplitude² is stated as the proportionality it is, with units on both quantities.
- The note's provenance is honest about how thin the source was: most of it is `student` with
  `ai-added` definitions, not a chapter's worth of `ai-added` material dressed up as the lesson.
