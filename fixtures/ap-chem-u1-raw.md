# AP Chem Unit 1

## 1.1

Atomic mass = molar mass
Atomic mass tells the

- Mass of 1 atom (in amu)
- Mass of 1 mole (in grams)

A mole is an amount

Atomic mass = mass of 1 mole

## 1.2

Atoms with the same number of protons but different number of neutrons are called isotopes.

- They will have different masses

Pair of isotopes can only be the same element

Relative abundance - the percentage of an isotope in a sample

Calculating the average atomic mass:1. Multiply the mass of each isotope by its percentage
2. Find the sum and divide by 100

Mass spectrometer
A machine that separates isotopes by mass and generates a graph

The graph
The y-axis is relative abundance (%).
The x-axis is mass (m/z)

## 1.3-1.4

Elements are pure substances, they're made of one type of atom
Some are monoatomic and exist as atoms
Other are diatomic and exist as molecules
The seven diatomic elements:

- Hydrogen
- Nitrogen
- Fluorine
- Oxygen
- Iodine
- Chlorine
- Bromine

Remember: Have No Fear of Ice Cold Beer

Diatomic elements have a subscript of 2 in their symbol

Compounds are pure substances that are formed by combining elements with a chemical bond.

A chemical bond is an attractive force between atoms in a compound.

Some compounds form individual molecules while others form crystals with repeating units. Repeatings units form crystals (lattice)

Formulas symbolize the ratio of elements within a substance
*For every 2 lithium atoms, there is 1 sulfur atom
*this ratio is constant
*this is the law of definite proportions

Molar mass from a formula
Add up the atomic mass of every atom in a compound -> the mass of 1 mole of that compound -> the mass of 6.02*1023 molecules of that compound

Molar mass of compounds can be used in dimensional analysis

Compounds containing only nonmetals form molecules
Compounds containing metals form units

Formulas express the ratio of elements in a chemical.
When the formula shows the true number of atoms its called a molecular formula.
When the formula is in lowest terms it is called an empirical formula.

The molecular formula is a multiple of an empirical formula
In some cases empirical formula is the true formula
In those cases the empirical and molecular formula are the same

With the molar mass and an empirical formula, a molecular formula is identifiable.
Given the empirical formula C5H7N, molar mass 162.26 g/mol
