# Bio - transport across the membrane

Membrane = phospholipid bilayer. Heads like water tails dont

Small nonpolar stuff goes straight through. Big or charged needs help

## passive

no energy
- diffusion - high to low
- facilitated diffusion - uses channel proteins
- osmosis - water

osmosis is the diffusion of water from low water potential to high water potential

isotonic hypertonic hypotonic
plant cell in hypotonic = turgid, animal cell = lyses

## active transport

needs ATP, low to high

sodium potassium pump - 3 Na out 2 K in, uses 1 ATP
this makes the inside more positive

endocytosis + exocytosis - vesicles. phagocytosis is cell eating
