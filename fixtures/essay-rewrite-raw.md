The Impact of Social Media on Teenage Mental Health

In today's modern society, social media has become a very big part of everyone's lives,
especially teenagers. Many people argue that social media is bad for teenagers and that it is
causing a mental health crisis. In this essay I will argue that social media is bad for teenage
mental health because of three reasons: comparison, sleep, and cyberbullying.

Firstly, social media causes teenagers to compare themselves to others. When you scroll through
Instagram you only see the best parts of other people's lives, which makes you feel bad about
your own life. This is called social comparison theory.

Secondly, social media affects sleep. Many teenagers stay up late on their phones and the blue
light stops them from sleeping properly, and sleep is very important for mental health.

Thirdly, cyberbullying has become much easier because of social media. Bullies can now reach
their victims at home which means there is no escape.

In conclusion, social media is bad for teenage mental health for the three reasons stated above
and something should be done about it.
